My research focuses on making learned robot policies **steerable** through human intervention. I am also interested in the human data behind these policies: how demonstrations, corrections, and preferences are collected and structured so that robots can learn from them efficiently.

I graduated with an MS from Carnegie Mellon University, where I worked on robotics with Prof. Jean Oh and Prof. Amir Barati Farimani. Before robotics, I worked in industry on a cardiac-arrest early-warning model for hospital rapid-response systems, and before that on subject-independent EEG decoding. I like contributing to open-source projects, including [RIO](https://robot-i-o.github.io/) and [OpenBMI](https://github.com/PatternRecognition/OpenBMI).
