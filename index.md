My research focuses on making learned robot policies **steerable** through human intervention. I am also interested in how to collect and structure human engagement data, such as demonstrations, corrections, and preferences, for sample-efficient robot learning.

I graduated with an MS from Carnegie Mellon University, where I worked on robotics with Prof. Jean Oh and Prof. Amir Barati Farimani. Before robotics, I worked on prediction from human physiological signals: in industry, a cardiac-arrest early-warning model, and before that, EEG decoding for brain–computer interfaces. I like contributing to open-source projects, including [RIO](https://robot-i-o.github.io/) and [OpenBMI](https://github.com/PatternRecognition/OpenBMI).
