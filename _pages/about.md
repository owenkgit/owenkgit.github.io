I graduated with an MS from Carnegie Mellon University, where I worked on robotics with Prof. Jean Oh and Prof. Amir Barati Farimani. My research spans robot learning, human-robot interaction, and decoding human signals. I am particularly interested in corrigibility in learned robot policies: correcting a policy's behavior while it runs, without retraining it, and in which human signals a policy can be corrected from and how few it needs.

Before robotics, I spent four years in industry building a cardiac-arrest early-warning model that ran in hospital rapid-response systems, and before that worked on subject-independent EEG decoding. I build in the open, contributing to [RIO](https://robot-i-o.github.io/) and [OpenBMI](https://github.com/PatternRecognition/OpenBMI), and releasing the experiments that worked, like [autosimulation](https://github.com/owenk3/autosimulation).
