I graduated with an MS from Carnegie Mellon University, focusing on robotics under the guidance of Prof. Jean Oh and Prof. Amir Barati Farimani.

I work on **connecting people to robots**—reading a human signal and turning it into machine action. Right now that means correcting Vision-Language-Action policies at inference time from plain language feedback, so a robot can be fixed mid-task by being told what it got wrong, without retraining. Earlier it meant predicting cardiac arrest from intermittent vital signs, and decoding motor imagery from EEG with an information-theoretic observation model. Different signals, same problem: infer what a person means, then act on it.

I also like building things in the open—contributing to [RIO](https://robot-i-o.github.io/) and [OpenBMI](https://github.com/PatternRecognition/OpenBMI), and releasing experiments that worked, like [autosimulation](https://github.com/owenk3/autosimulation).
