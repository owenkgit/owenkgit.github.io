My research focuses on making learned robot policies **steerable** by people while they run. I am also interested in collecting and structuring human feedback data for robot learning.

I graduated with an MS from Carnegie Mellon University, where I worked on robotics with Prof. Jean Oh and Prof. Amir Barati Farimani. Before robotics, I worked on prediction from human physiological signals: in industry, a cardiac-arrest early-warning model, and before that, EEG decoding for brain–computer interfaces.
